Quick help

Everything runs in this browser. No account, server or upload required.

Get started

  1. Open Roster and add members with a photo or a guided multi-angle capture.
  2. Return to Live scanner and start the camera. Allow camera access when prompted.
  3. Members stand in view until the scanner verifies a match across frames and logs the check-in.
  4. Use Logs & analytics to review, filter and export attendance.

If recognition struggles

Use a well-lit, steady camera at the check-in station and follow the on-screen framing guidance. If check-in does not work, use manual check-in rather than trying to identify someone from a distance or while they are walking past.

Privacy

Camera frames are processed in memory and discarded. Face templates, photos and attendance logs live in this browser's local storage. Export a JSON backup before clearing browser data or changing devices.
